Frequently Asked Questions

Is MiningFlow free?

Yes. MiningFlow is an open-source, ad-free calculator. No account or payment is required to use it.

Where does the data come from?

BTC price, GMT price, and network difficulty are fetched live from public APIs (CoinPaprika, CoinGecko, and mempool.space). If an API fails, MiningFlow falls back to sensible default values.

Is the profit estimate guaranteed?

No. MiningFlow gives an estimate based on current BTC price, difficulty, and GoMining's fee structure. Actual results depend on network difficulty changes, BTC price volatility, and GoMining's terms.

What is "Pay in GMT"?

Paying fees in GMT gives an 8% reduction on gross electricity and service fees. Toggle it to see how it changes your net profit.

What is the click streak bonus?

GoMining rewards daily logins with a small loyalty boost. MiningFlow approximates this as a 0.5% daily-net increase when the toggle is enabled.

How does the Capital Planner work?

The Capital Planner runs a greedy allocator across four strategies — locking GMT, buying 15 W/TH upgrades, buying 12 W/TH miners, and upgrading efficiency — and picks the combination that gives the highest monthly uplift for your budget.

Can I share my setup?

Yes. Click "Copy share link" in the Your Setup panel to copy a URL that includes your current inputs. Anyone opening that link will see the same setup.

How do I support MiningFlow?
