GoMining ROI & Break-Even Calculator

Return on investment with GoMining depends on more than the headline price per TH. To know your real payback you need to account for electricity, service fees, conversion fees, discounts, Bitcoin price and network difficulty. MiningFlow does all of this live.

How GoMining ROI works

ROI is simply your net profit divided by your invested capital. Net profit is daily revenue minus all fees. Invested capital is what you paid for the TH plus the GMT you lock to get the discount, because that capital is also at work.

The variables that actually move it

  • Bitcoin price: rewards are paid in BTC, so a higher BTC price raises revenue in dollar terms while fixed fees stay the same.
  • Network difficulty: as difficulty rises, each TH earns fewer sats per day.
  • Halving: every four years the block subsidy is cut in half, reducing revenue unless BTC price rises to compensate.
  • Efficiency (W/TH): lower efficiency means lower electricity fees and faster break-even.
  • Discount stack: VIP tier + locked GMT discount directly reduce fees and shorten payback.

Why a projection beats a flat estimate

A flat estimate assumes BTC price and difficulty never change. In reality they do. MiningFlow's Growth Projection runs a multi-year forecast with difficulty growth and halving cycles so you can see how break-even moves under different scenarios.

Frequently asked questions

Does break-even include the GMT I lock?

Yes. MiningFlow counts locked GMT at the current GMT price as part of your invested capital, because that capital is tied up in the discount program instead of being available elsewhere.

What happens if Bitcoin price drops?

Your revenue drops in dollar terms, but fees stay fixed, so break-even extends. This is why we model scenarios rather than promising a single number.

Can I improve ROI after buying?

Yes. Upgrading efficiency, locking more GMT, reaching a higher VIP tier or reinvesting rewards can all shorten payback.
