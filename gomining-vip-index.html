GoMining VIP Tiers & Benefits

GoMining VIP tiers reward larger operators with bigger electricity-fee discounts and extra perks. You qualify by your total hashrate (TH) or the amount of GMT you lock, whichever places you higher. Here is the complete list of tiers and what each one gives you.

How VIP tiers work

There are two ways to climb the VIP ladder:

  • Hashrate path: own enough total TH on the account.
  • GMT path: lock enough GMT to qualify at the equivalent level.

Your tier is determined by whichever metric is higher. The discount stacks with the token discount from locked GMT, giving you two separate levers to lower fees.

Frequently asked questions

What are GoMining VIP tiers?

GoMining VIP tiers are status levels you reach by holding hashrate (TH) or locked GMT. Each tier unlocks a larger electricity-fee discount and extra perks like referral bonuses.

How do you qualify for a higher VIP tier?

You qualify by total hashrate or locked GMT, whichever is higher. For example, 100 TH or 1,000 locked GMT may qualify for Gold I.

What is the maximum VIP discount?

The highest Elite tier unlocks a 6% VIP discount on electricity fees, stacked on top of the up to 20% token discount from locked GMT.
